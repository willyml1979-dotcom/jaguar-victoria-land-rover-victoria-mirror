Every journey feels special in our 2019 Land Rover Discovery Sport HSE Luxury P240 AWD, finished in elegant Corris Grey Metallic and equipped with the Black Pack! Powered by a turbocharged 2.0L 4-cylinder engine delivering 237 horsepower and paired with a 9-speed automatic transmission, this SUV combines refined performance with impressive all-weather capability. Enjoy fuel economy of approximately 9.3 L/100 km on the highway, along with Land Rovers Terrain Response system for added confidence on challenging terrain. Distinctive styling highlights include a panoramic sunroof, xenon headlamps with LED signature lighting, a contrasting black roof, and stylish black alloy wheels. Inside, enjoy Windsor leather seating, heated power front seats with driver memory, a heated multifunction steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, configurable ambient lighting, and a convenient power gesture-operated liftgate. Stay connected with touchscreen navigation, Bluetooth, USB connectivity, and premium Meridian audio. A rearview camera, front and rear parking sensors, rain-sensing wipers, and available driver-assistance features help enhance confidence on every drive. Combining upscale comfort, versatile capability, and unmistakable Land Rover style, this Discovery Sport HSE Luxury is ready for your next adventure! Save this page and call for availability.
