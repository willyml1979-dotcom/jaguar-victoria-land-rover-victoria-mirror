Our stunning 2021 Land Rover Range Rover Velar S AWD combines rugged trail capability and a refined road presence in Fuji White! Motivated by a TurboCharged 3.0 Litre 6 Cylinder delivering 335hp matched to a paddle-shifted 8 Speed Automatic transmission. This All Wheel Drive SUV also boasts a top-notch terrain management system for off-road adventures, and it scores approximately 9.0L/100km on the highway with mild-hybrid assistance. A stunning design is standard as well, with LED lighting, a panoramic sunroof, a power tailgate, flush door handles, power-folding heated mirrors, and bold alloy wheels. Visit our Velar S cabin and enjoy first-class accommodations with grained leather power heated front seats, a power leather steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, and dual touchscreens, too. They're part of a high-tech infotainment setup that serves you right with Android Auto, Apple CarPlay, Bluetooth, a WiFi hotspot, full-color navigation, and an 11-speaker Meridian sound system. Ample cargo space adds further functionality to this well-crafted interior! Land Rover's smart safety measures let you explore your world with peace of mind, knowing you can count on a blind-spot monitor, driver attention monitor, lane-keeping assistance, parking sensors, and more.
